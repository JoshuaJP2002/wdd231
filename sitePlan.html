Site Plan: Explore Dominican Republic

Site Name

Explore Dominican Republic

This name was chosen because it clearly represents a website focused on tourism and exploring different places in the Dominican Republic.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide information about popular tourist destinations in the Dominican Republic. The site will help users learn about places to visit, activities, and basic travel tips.

Scenarios

  • What are the best places to visit in the Dominican Republic?
  • What activities can I do in Punta Cana or Samaná?

Color Scheme

Blue (#005f99): Used for headings and highlights (represents ocean and sky).

Green (#00a86b): Used for accents and borders (represents nature and landscapes).

Typography

Poppins: Used for headings.

Arial: Used for body text.
